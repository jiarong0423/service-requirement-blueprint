## One conflict, eight workflow beats

1. **Expectation gap:** a Taiwan NT$199 one-page website offer inspires the opening. The client expects mobile layout, a form, deployment, and unlimited revisions; the contractor initially offers one static page, client-provided content, and one revision. This is a de-identified narrative illustration, not a product currency default or a checkout.
2. **Clarify:** the client saves JSON A as the requirement baseline.
3. **Discuss and revise:** an illustrated negotiation introduces the disagreement; actual synthetic case records then show messages, JSON B, explicit inclusions, exclusions, fee items, and acceptance checks. The revised form and mobile button are included; deployment and unlimited revisions are excluded.
4. **Resolve:** the contractor proposes treatment and the client explicitly accepts it. Revision alone does not close the issue.
5. **Confirm:** one party remains pending until the other confirms the same version. The shared PDF and workflow diagram preserve the fixed baseline.
6. **Return:** the demonstration records a passing form check and a failing mobile-button check, with the client's stated correction request.
7. **Correct and inspect again:** the contractor records correction evidence; reinspection preserves the earlier return. The client explicitly accepts both current items. Silence and deadlines never create consent.
8. **Preserve evidence:** the ZIP, PDF, version records, delivery, inspection, acceptance, and timeline are shown as verified artifacts. The platform does not decide who is right.

## Evidence limits

- The case, offer, messages, tests, and correction references are synthetic demonstration material. They are not proof of an independently tested or deployed third-party website at 390 pixels.
- Scenes 1, 2, and 6 are illustrations. Scenes 3, 9, 17, and 18 are recorded application video. The other 16 scenes use still images, including product screens and verified artifact cards.
- PDF and ZIP contents were inspected off camera. The edit shows stills of verified outputs; it does not claim to film the ZIP download click.
- Sign-in, private invitation handling, account administration, and the separate dispute branch are supported by the interactive guide and tests; they are not all shown in this final edit.
- Mechanical subtitle, audio timing, decoding, and picture checks have receipts. Continuous human playback and listening remain a separate review before video publication.
- This source repository excludes recordings, case files, private accounts, invitation secrets, learner profile, and internal production logs. A source manifest proves source identity, not audiovisual or Devpost submission approval.
